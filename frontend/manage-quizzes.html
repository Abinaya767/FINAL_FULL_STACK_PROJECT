Student Learning Flow

  1. Student completes the lessons in Module 1 and takes the Module 1 quiz.
  2. After passing, the next module becomes available with its own quiz.
  3. This continues for every module in the selected course.
  4. After all modules are completed and their quizzes are passed, the student can take the separate final overall assessment.
  5. A certificate is issued only after the student passes the final overall assessment.
