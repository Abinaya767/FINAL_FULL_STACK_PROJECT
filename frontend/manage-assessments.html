Manage Final Assessments

Create and manage a final assessment for each course. Students can access it only after completing every lesson and passing every module quiz.

Each assessment must contain 20–30 questions. Every question has four options. The final assessment has no two-attempt or 24-hour module-quiz limit.

Assessment Rules

  • Each course can have one final assessment.
  • Students must complete every lesson and pass every module quiz first.
  • Assessment answers are checked securely by the server.
  • Final assessment attempts are recorded in the database and are not limited by the module quiz cooldown.
  • A certificate is issued only after the student passes the final assessment.
