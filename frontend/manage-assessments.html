Assessment Management Rules

  • Each course can have its own final assessment.
  • Students must complete all course lessons and pass every module quiz before accessing the final assessment.
  • The administrator configures total marks and passing criteria.
  • Assessment answers must be graded securely on the server.
  • Student marks and results must be stored in MongoDB.
  • Students who pass become eligible for a course completion certificate.
  • Certificate generation must happen only after successful verification of the result.
