Manage Courses

Create, update, view and manage all courses available on the LearnSpace platform.

Add New Course














Enter at least 10 modules. Each module needs a title, at least one lesson with a title and content, and a quiz with exactly five questions. Every question must use the "question" field, exactly four non-empty options, and a correctAnswerIndex from 0 to 3 (A to D). Each module quiz allows two attempts; failing both starts a 24-hour cooldown.
